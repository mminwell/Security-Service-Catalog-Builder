Site Admin is limited to the platform owner. A workspace Admin cannot open this page. Deactivate is the usual way to stop someone signing in, and it can be reversed. Permanent deletion asks you to type the email.

Deleted workspaces lists the history that remains after a workspace is removed. Each line says when it happened, who did it, the workspace name, and what changed, in plain language. Role changes, invitations, ownership transfers, and the deletion itself stay readable there.
