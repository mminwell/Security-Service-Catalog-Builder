Workspace settings holds the company name, logo, service ID style, distribution lines, export defaults, members, and invites. The slug does not change when you change the company name.

An Admin invites someone by email and chooses the role. The person does not pick their own role. Someone without an account follows the invite link to finish the account: the email is locked, and the invited role is applied when they save a name and password. Someone who already has an account signs in, and the invite is accepted for them. Invites expire after seven days and can be resent or cancelled. Removing a member takes them out of this workspace only. It does not delete the account. The last Admin cannot be removed.

Viewer can read and export. Editor can change services, artifacts, and assessments. Service Manager can edit only the services they own. Admin manages the workspace. Platform owner is the Site Admin, the person who can reach every workspace.

Transfer ownership is in the Danger zone, and only the Site Admin can see that section. Choose a current member, or enter an email for someone who is not in this workspace yet. A new email becomes the owner only after they accept. Until then the current owner stays the owner. After the transfer, the previous owner is an Admin.

Delete is in the same Danger zone. The Site Admin types the company name exactly, then the workspace and its data are removed.

Change history describes those events in plain language, along with service and settings edits. Open it from the catalog. After a workspace is deleted, the remaining history is on the Site Admin page under Deleted workspaces.
