The catalog lists the services in the current workspace. Search and filters apply to the rows you see. Archived services stay out of the list until you include them.

Admins can choose and save the columns. Everyone else can keep a column arrangement in this browser.

Services with an archived or missing owner can be filtered as Owner needed. Open a row to edit that service.
