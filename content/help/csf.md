CSF Mapping shows NIST CSF 2.0 functions, categories, and outcomes. You record coverage on each outcome: Full, Partial, or None. A count is an explicit assessment on an active service.

Each category heading shows how many of that category's outcomes are Full, Partial, None, and Unmapped. Those badges add up to the outcomes in the category. They do not change how you map a service. Mapping stays on the individual outcome.

Open an outcome from a service to record the assessment, a rationale, and notes. Unmapped means no assessment yet. The primary function dropdown on the service form is not coverage. Archived services are not coverage either.
