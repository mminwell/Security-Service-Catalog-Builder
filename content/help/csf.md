CSF Mapping shows NIST CSF 2.0 functions, categories, and outcomes. A count is an explicit assessment on an active service: full, partial, or none.

Open an outcome from a service to record the assessment, a rationale, and notes. Unmapped means no assessment yet. The primary function dropdown on the service form is not coverage.
