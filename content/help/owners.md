Service Owners is the directory of people who can be named on a service. Add someone from the workspace members, which fills in their name and email, or add a person who has no account.

The display name is generated as Last, First and is not typed. Remove archives the person and asks for a replacement, or flags those services as Owner needed. The record is kept.

Reconcile compares the directory with an active-staff or termination list. Match by email first, then by name. Ambiguous names are not archived automatically.
