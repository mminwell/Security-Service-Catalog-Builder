Try the demo opens a fictional catalog with no sign-in. You can add, edit, delete, map controls, and export. Nothing is written to the database.

A banner stays on screen: Demo mode, nothing is saved. Refreshing or closing the tab restores the sample services. Create an account when you want a workspace that keeps your changes.

The demo does not send email, and it does not open Site Admin.
