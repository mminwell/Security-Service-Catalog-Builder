A service is one offering your team delivers. Core fields are the service code, name, and the short description the catalog needs to stay identifiable.

Depth switches among Core, Operations, and Governance. Hiding a depth does not clear the values.

Service Owner is a searchable list of active people from the workspace directory. The name is always shown as Last, First. New people are added on the Service Owners page, not typed into the form.

Archive hides a service. Permanent delete is an Admin action.
