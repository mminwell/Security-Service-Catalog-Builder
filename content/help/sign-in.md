Sign in with your email and password, or choose Email me a sign-in link.

The link is a one-time magic link. It expires after a short time and cannot be used again. The confirmation is the same whether or not that email already has an account. Password sign-in, password reset, and Create account stay on the same page.

If the email and password do not match, the page says so. Too many attempts in a short period asks you to wait a few minutes and try again. If sign-in does not start a session, try again. A deactivated account cannot sign in until a Site Admin reactivates it.

Create account asks for your first name, last name, email, and password. There is no Company box. The first account becomes Admin of a workspace named from the first name, such as "Ada's workspace". When a first name is not available, the name is "My workspace". Rename it later in Workspace settings. The company name there is the name people see. Changing it does not change the short address of the workspace.
