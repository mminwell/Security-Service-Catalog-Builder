Sign in with your email and password, or choose Email me a sign-in link.

The link is a one-time magic link. It expires after a short time and cannot be used again. Password sign-in, password reset, and Create account stay on the same page.

The confirmation is the same whether or not that email has an account. A deactivated account cannot sign in until a Site Admin reactivates it.

Create account asks for your first name, last name, and password. The workspace created with the first account starts as a placeholder name, such as "Ada's workspace", or "My workspace" when a first name is not available. Rename it later in Workspace Settings.
